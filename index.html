Questions, answered.

Does the person need a crypto wallet?

No. After they confirm their email, they can paste any Solana address or create a brand-new wallet in their browser. We show them the private key once so they can import it into Phantom or Solflare.

What do I need to send?

A Solana wallet in your browser — Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. On a phone, open this site inside your wallet app's browser.

What if I type the wrong email?

Open your tracking link and cancel. Any send that hasn't been claimed goes straight back to the wallet you sent from.

What if they never open it?

After 30 days it's returned to your wallet automatically.

What does it cost?

There's no service fee. You pay Solana network fees, a fraction of a cent for SOL. USDC and USDT sends also set aside about 0.004 SOL to open token accounts; about half of that comes back to you when the send closes.

Who can see the coins while they wait?

The escrow is a normal Solana wallet, so anyone can see its balance on the explorer. Moving the coins needs the escrow key, which is encrypted on our servers and only used to release funds to the verified recipient or back to you.
